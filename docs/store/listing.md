## Indonesia (bahasa default)

**Judul (30):** `Sellby Keyboard`

**Deskripsi singkat (80):** `Keyboard bisnis: bikin invoice, cek ongkir & status pesanan dari chat` (69 karakter)

**Deskripsi lengkap:**

```
Sellby Keyboard adalah keyboard untuk seller online. Bikin invoice, cek ongkir, kelola status pesanan, produk, dan balasan cepat langsung dari keyboard, tanpa pindah-pindah aplikasi saat melayani pembeli di chat.

FITUR DI TOOLBAR KEYBOARD
- Invoice: susun teks tagihan dari data pembeli, produk (katalog atau input manual), ekspedisi, ongkir, dan metode pembayaran (termasuk foto QRIS), lalu kirim ke chat. Form Order yang disalin dari chat pembeli bisa langsung ditempel ke invoice.
- Ongkir: daftar kurir dengan jalan pintas ke aplikasi atau situs cek ongkir.
- Status: lacak pesanan Pending, Lunas, Proses, dan Selesai, dengan pesan status siap kirim.
- Produk: katalog produk lengkap dengan stok, diskon, dan variasi; kirim info produk ke chat.
- Auto-Text: pesan cepat dengan shortcut dan saran saat mengetik.
- Kalkulator, pengaturan ukuran keyboard, dan mode gelap.

APLIKASI PENDAMPING
- Dashboard ringkasan penjualan dan pelanggan
- Ekspor data transaksi dan pelanggan ke Excel
- Tutorial interaktif langkah demi langkah

PRIVASI
- Apa yang kamu ketik diproses hanya di HP-mu dan tidak dikirim ke server.
- Data pelanggan, pesanan, produk, dan rekening disimpan di HP-mu.
- Tanpa iklan. Tanpa akun.

HARGA
Gratis dicoba 3 hari dengan semua fitur. Setelah itu fitur panel dibuka dengan sekali bayar lewat Google Play, tanpa langganan.

OPEN SOURCE
Sellby adalah perangkat lunak bebas berlisensi GPL-3.0, turunan dari HeliBoard. Kode sumber lengkap ada di layar Tentang & Lisensi di dalam aplikasi.

Nama dan logo bank, e-wallet, kurir, serta aplikasi chat milik pemiliknya masing-masing; Sellby tidak berafiliasi dengan mereka.
```

**Catatan rilis (versi 1.0.0):** `Rilis pertama Sellby Keyboard: invoice, ongkir, status pesanan, produk, Auto-Text, kalkulator, dan aplikasi pendamping.`

## English (translation)

**Title (30):** `Sellby Keyboard`

**Short description (80):** `Business keyboard: invoices, shipping & order status inside your chats` (70 characters)

**Full description:**

```
Sellby Keyboard is a keyboard for online sellers. Build invoices, check shipping, and manage order status, products and quick replies right from the keyboard, without switching apps while you serve buyers in chat.

KEYBOARD TOOLBAR FEATURES
- Invoice: compose the bill text from buyer details, products (catalog or manual entry), courier, shipping cost and payment method (including a QRIS photo), then send it to the chat. An order form copied from the buyer's chat can be pasted straight into the invoice.
- Shipping: a list of couriers with shortcuts to their apps or sites to check shipping costs.
- Status: track Pending, Paid, Processing and Done orders, with ready-to-send status messages.
- Products: a product catalog with stock, discounts and variants; send product info to the chat.
- Auto-Text: quick replies with shortcuts and suggestions while you type.
- Calculator, keyboard size setting and dark mode.

COMPANION APP
- Sales and customer summary dashboard
- Export transactions and customers to Excel
- Interactive step-by-step tutorial

PRIVACY
- What you type is processed only on your phone and is not sent to any server.
- Customer, order, product and bank details are stored on your phone.
- No ads. No account.

PRICE
Free to try for 3 days with every feature. After that the panel features unlock with a one-time purchase through Google Play, no subscription.

OPEN SOURCE
Sellby is free software under GPL-3.0, derived from HeliBoard. The complete source is linked on the "Tentang & Lisensi" (About & License) screen in the app.

Names and logos of banks, e-wallets, couriers and chat apps belong to their respective owners; Sellby is not affiliated with them.
```

**Release notes (1.0.0):** `First release of Sellby Keyboard: invoices, shipping, order status, products, Auto-Text, calculator and a companion app.`
