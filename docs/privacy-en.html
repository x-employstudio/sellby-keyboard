Sellby Keyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · applies to Sellby Keyboard 1.0.x (package com.sellby.keyboard)

In short: what you type is processed only on your phone. Sellby has no server that receives your keystrokes, customers, orders, products or bank details. All of that stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere by us.

1. Who we are

Sellby Keyboard is an Android keyboard for online sellers, developed by the owner of the Sellby project ("we"). Privacy contact: x.employstudio@gmail.com.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not record, transmit or sell what you type.
  • We use no advertising or analytics SDKs and show no ads.
  • We do not create user accounts or ask for your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • We operate no server that stores your store's data.

4. Permissions the app requests

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): requested once, the first time you tap the contact button in the Invoice panel, so contact picking behaves the same on every phone brand. The app only reads the contact you tap in the list. Denying the permission does not stop you from entering buyer details by hand.
  • Vibrate and Run at startup: for key vibration and to prepare the keyboard after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: declared by the Google Play Billing library (see section 5). The keyboard does not use them to send what you type.

5. Google services used

The app uses Google services that are governed by Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Google Play Billing: the one-time premium purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your card number or payment details; the app only learns the purchase status. The library may send usage logs to Google, outside our control.
  • Google Play In-App Review: shows a Play Store review request on day 3 of the trial.
  • Block Store (Google Play services): keeps a copy of the trial start time outside the app's own data, so the 3-day trial applies once per device and does not restart after a reinstall. Only one timestamp is stored, with no personal data.
  • Android Auto Backup (Google Drive): if your phone's backup is on, Android may back up the app's data (store data, QRIS photos, settings, trial/purchase status) to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by your phone's backup settings and Google, not by us. Clipboard history and dictionaries are not backed up.

6. Data you share yourself

Some features send data out only when you act: sending invoice/status text to WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram or Instagram; opening a courier's website or app to check shipping costs; saving an Excel export to a location you choose with the system file picker. After that, the data is governed by the privacy policy of the destination app or service.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete All Data (Settings in the keyboard): removes customers, orders, products, payment methods, QRIS photos, store profile and templates. The trial marker and purchase status are deliberately kept so the trial cannot be repeated and a purchase is not lost.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data from the phone. Copies in your Google backup can be removed in your Google account's backup settings.
  • Because your store's data is not on our servers, we cannot see or delete it for you; you control it directly on your phone.

8. Children

Sellby Keyboard is meant for sellers aged 16 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by the Android system and your screen lock. No storage method is completely risk-free; keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example when a new feature sends data to a server), we will update this page and its date before the affected app version is released.
